FlyWLPNet
Vesper runs the real 4,010-parameter network — a 3 → 64 → 56 → 2 ReLU policy trained by REINFORCE over 200,000 hands — evaluated in your browser, weight for weight. Over 10,000 hands it averages −0.049 per hand against an exact optimum of −0.0466. Iris is the same mushroom body fed a one-hot dealer upcard instead of upcard/10, which stops an ace reading as the weakest card on the axis: 4,586 weights, −0.046 per hand, and agreement with the optimum up from 88.4% to 92.4%. Nova is that same architecture with the training loop still attached: she starts from random weights and runs REINFORCE in your browser — return-to-go, a running-mean baseline, an entropy bonus, Adam decaying from 5e-4 to 2e-5 — reaching roughly 88% agreement in about 200,000 hands, which takes under a minute here. Dizzy is the same architecture trained by the original specification’s broken loop, which collapsed to always-hit — held to one house rule here, that he stands on 21 rather than hitting a hand he cannot improve.
